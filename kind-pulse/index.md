## PRIVACY POLICY — KINDPULSE

**In short: KindPulse keeps everything on your phone. It has no accounts and no
servers. We never see your information unless you choose to send us a problem
report.**

## What we collect

Nothing automatically. There are no KindPulse accounts, no analytics, no ads,
and no tracking. We — the people who made KindPulse — never receive anything
you put into the app.

We only receive something when you choose to send it:

- **A problem report.** Settings → Report a problem opens an email in your own
  email app. It holds what you typed under "What happened?", the app and Android
  versions, your phone's model, and a log of errors the app recorded. The log
  never contains your reminders, names or contacts. You can read and change the
  whole email before sending it, and nothing is sent unless you press send. Like
  any email, it reaches us from your email address, which we use only to reply.
  We use reports only to find and fix problems, and we never share them.
- **A donation.** Google Play gives us a record that a donation was made. See
  Donations below.

## Your information stays on your phone

Everything you create — your reminders, calendars, the people they are for, and
your history — is saved on your phone, in an area that only KindPulse can open.
If you set an app lock, its PIN is kept in your phone's encrypted secure
storage.

A few more things we would rather tell you than not:

- **A reminder that is waiting to notify you.** So that it still arrives even if
  you restart your phone, your phone keeps its own copy of that notification's
  wording — the person's name, and what you planned to do. It is removed as soon
  as the notification has been shown, or when you complete or delete the
  reminder.
- **Simple settings**, such as your chosen theme, your snooze length, and
  whether you have been through the welcome screens. These say nothing about you
  or about the people you care about.

- **Your phone's own backup.** If your phone's Google backup is turned on,
  Android copies KindPulse's reminders, calendars and settings to your Google
  account, as it does for other apps, so they come back on a new phone. Your
  photos and your app lock are not included. Google keeps this copy, and we
  cannot see it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

KindPulse only goes online for things you set up yourself:

- to import from a service you sign in to (see Importing from other apps
  below);
- to read a calendar you added from a web address. The app asks that address
  for the calendar's events, about once a day, and sends it nothing else;
- for a donation, through Google Play.

Nothing you enter into the app is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

## Your contacts

If you allow it, KindPulse reads your contacts on your phone. This lets you link
a reminder to a person, and lets the app notice birthdays.

- Your contacts are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
- The app only remembers a person's name, their ID on your phone, and any dates
  saved on the contact, such as a birthday, so it knows who a reminder is for.
- Contact photos are never saved. They are shown straight from your phone each
  time.
- This is optional. You can type names in yourself and use the whole app without
  giving contact access.

## Your phone's calendars

If you allow it, KindPulse reads the calendars on your phone and copies their
events into the app, so they sit beside your reminders. It only reads them: it
never changes or deletes anything in your phone's calendars, and the copies stay
on your phone. This is optional too.

## Importing from other apps

You can bring reminders and events in from other apps. A file you import, such
as another app's backup or export, is read on your phone.

If you choose to sign in to Google, Microsoft or Todoist to import from them,
KindPulse talks to that service directly from your phone. It reads your lists,
tasks and calendars once, to copy them into the app. It never changes anything
in your account, and it forgets the sign-in as soon as the import is done.
Nothing passes through us.

KindPulse's use and transfer to any other app of information received from
Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including
the Limited Use requirements.

## Photos

If you add a photo to a reminder, KindPulse keeps a small copy of it inside the
app, on your phone. When a memory collage is ready, the app saves it to your
phone's Pictures folder and then deletes those copies. Photos are never
uploaded. They leave your phone only if you share or export them yourself.

## Notifications

Reminders appear through your phone's own notifications. Nothing about them leaves
your phone.

Like any other app's notifications, a reminder can appear on your lock screen. If
you would rather it did not, your phone's own notification settings let you hide
notification contents there.

## Backups you make

KindPulse lets you save a backup of your reminders. The backup file is
encrypted, and it is saved wherever you choose on your device. It belongs to you.
We never see it and have no way to reach it.

If you move it somewhere else, such as a cloud drive, it then also follows that
service's rules. And if you lose it, we cannot get it back for you, so keep it
safe.

## Donations

Donations are optional. KindPulse is free, and nothing in it is locked behind a
payment.

If you choose to donate, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never
see your card or payment details. Google Play only gives us a record that the
donation was made.

## Deleting everything

Uninstalling KindPulse removes everything it saved inside the app on your
phone. Collages already saved to your Pictures folder stay there, like your
other photos. A copy in your phone's Google backup follows Google's rules for
backups. We hold nothing else of yours, apart from any problem report you
emailed us, and we will delete that if you ask.

## Children

KindPulse is not aimed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear at this same web address,
with a new date at the bottom.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact the developer.
